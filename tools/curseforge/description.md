# Azeroth After Dark on CurseForge

## Summary (one line)

Daily WoW: Forever news, hotfixes and screenshots, right in your game.

## Description

**The WoW: Forever news desk, inside your game.**

Azeroth After Dark brings the day's WoW: Forever news to you the moment you log in. No alt-tabbing, no digging
through sites. Hotfixes, maintenance, events, new discoveries and community buzz, written up short and clear
by the team behind the Azeroth After Dark show.

### What you get

- **A front page on login.** When there's a story you haven't seen, the news window opens with the top story up
  front and every other headline beside it.
- **Read it in game.** Click any headline for the full write-up, with pictures you can flip through.
- **Hotfixes made readable.** Hotfix stories come with a clear "What changed" list, class by class.
- **Sources always linked.** Every story credits where it came from (Blizzard, Wowhead, Icy Veins), with a link
  you can copy into your browser.
- **Minimap button** with an unread count, so you know when there's something new.
- **Fresh every day.** A new edition is published whenever there's news. Keep the addon updated through the
  CurseForge app to get it.

### Commands

- `/aad` opens or closes the news (also `/afterdark` and `/wownews`)
- `/aad popup new` / `always` / `off` sets when it opens on login (default: only when there's a new story)
- `/aad minimap` shows or hides the minimap button
- `/aad reset` moves the window back to the middle of the screen

### Good to know

- Addons can't go online, so each day's news ships inside the addon. Update it to get the latest edition.
  If your copy is more than a few days old, the window will tell you.
- After an update, **restart the game** rather than just using /reload, so the new pictures load.
- Made for **WoW: Forever**.

Watch the show at [wow.afterdark.fm](https://wow.afterdark.fm). Open source on
